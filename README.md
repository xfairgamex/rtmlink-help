# Welcome to RTMLink Help

RTMLink helps your clinic run its Remote Therapeutic Monitoring (RTM) program — enrolling patients, sending daily check-in surveys, tracking progress between visits, and turning that activity into clean, billable claims.

This help center is written for the people who use RTMLink every day: **clinic owners, providers, front-desk staff, billing staff, and auditors.** Every guide is task-focused — open one, follow the steps, get back to your patients.

## New to RTMLink?

Start with **Getting Started** in the sidebar. It covers signing in, finding your way around, and what your role can do.

## Find what you need

Use the sidebar to jump to a topic:

* **Getting Started**: sign in, the dashboard, and your role.
* **Patients**: adding patients, consent, and keeping records up to date.
* **Episodes**: enrolling a patient in RTM, pausing, and closing.
* **Surveys**: sending check-ins, reading responses, and what counts toward billing.
* **Exercises**: assigning home-exercise programs and tracking completion.
* **Messages, Billing, Settings, and more**: everything else your clinic manages day to day.

## Can't find an answer?

If something here is unclear or out of date, tell your clinic administrator so we can fix it. This help center is kept in step with the app as it grows.

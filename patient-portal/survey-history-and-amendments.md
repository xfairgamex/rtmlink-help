# Survey history and amendments

Patients can look back at their recent check-ins and, if they answered something wrong, correct it, all from the same link they use each day. This article covers the patient's side; for how corrections appear to your team, see [Survey amendments](../check-ins/survey-amendments.md).

## Viewing history

From the thank-you page or a check-in, the patient taps **View All History** to open **Survey History**: a list of their recent days. Each day shows either **Completed** with an **Edit** button, or **Available** with a **Take Survey** button for a day they have not done yet. A patient who is brand new simply sees that no surveys are available yet.

## Correcting a past response

Opening a completed day shows the patient their answers. If the day is still correctable, they see **Amend Response**:

1. The patient taps **Amend Response**.
2. RTMLink opens a copy of that check-in, pre-filled with their original answers, and reassures them that the original is preserved.
3. They fix what they need and submit, exactly like a normal check-in.

The original answer is never overwritten; the amendment is saved alongside it and re-enters your team's review queue as a fresh, unread response. A response shows an **Amended** label once it has been corrected.

> A response can only be amended while the patient's current billing window is still open. Older check-ins show "This response is from a previous billing window and cannot be amended" instead of the button.

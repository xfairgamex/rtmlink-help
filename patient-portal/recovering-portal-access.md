# Recovering portal access

Patients occasionally lose their check-in link or find it stopped working. RTMLink has a self-service way back in, and it is worth knowing so you can point a patient to it quickly.

## Requesting a fresh check-in link

Most clinics use magic links, where the texted or emailed link is all a patient needs. If a patient's link has expired or they deleted the message, they can request a new one:

1. Send them to your clinic's **Get a fresh check-in link** page.
2. They enter the **Phone number** your clinic has on file for them.
3. RTMLink texts a brand-new link, and the page confirms with **Check your phone**.

The number must match the mobile number on the patient's record. If it does not, update the patient's phone in their record first, then have them try again.

## Resetting a portal password

Clinics that use a full password portal have a **Reset your password** page instead: the patient enters the **Phone number** on file, receives a reset link by text, and chooses a new password. A **Back to sign in** link returns them to the login screen.

## Expired sessions

If a patient leaves the portal open and idle, they may see a **Session Expired** message. This is normal security behavior; they simply reopen their latest link (or request a fresh one as above) to continue.

## When to step in

If a patient cannot receive the text at all, the issue is usually the phone number on file or a texting block. Check the number on their patient record and whether their line can receive texts.

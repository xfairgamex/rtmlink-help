# Sending messages

You can message a patient from the Messages inbox or from the **Message** buttons scattered where you already work: the patient list and record, the episode page, and the daily review screen. Both paths use the same composer and land in the same shared inbox.

## Starting a new conversation

1. In **Messages**, click **New Message**.
2. Type in the **Search for a patient** box and pick the patient. Results show which channels each patient can receive: **SMS available**, **Email available**, or both.
3. Choose the channel from **Select Channel**.
4. Type your message and click **Send**.

A patient's channels depend on their contact info and consent. A channel that is unavailable tells you why when you hover it: no phone number on file, no email address on file, or the patient has not consented to that channel.

## The composer

- Texts are capped at 1,600 characters, with a live counter under the box.
- Email messages add a subject line (replies pre-fill it with "Re:" and the prior subject).
- Enter sends the message; Shift+Enter starts a new line.
- There are no attachments; messages are text only.

![A conversation open in Messages: outbound bubbles with Sent, Delivered, and Read labels, patient replies on the left.](../.gitbook/assets/messaging/messages-inbox.png)

## Delivery status

Each outbound message shows its journey under the bubble: **Sending**, then **Sent**, **Delivered**, and **Read** where the channel reports it. A message that could not be delivered shows **Not delivered** in red; see [Managing conversations](managing-conversations.md) for what to do next.

## Message buttons around the app

- **Patients**: each row's **Message** action, and the button on the patient record.
- **Episodes**: the episode list and episode page actions.
- **Daily review**: each patient row's **Message Patient** button, which also carries a red badge when the patient has unread messages.

These open a slide-over composer with the same channel pills, statuses, and scheduling as the inbox, so you never lose your place.

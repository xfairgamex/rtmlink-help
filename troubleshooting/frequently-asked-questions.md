# Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions that come up most. Each links to the fuller guide.

## What counts as an interaction day?

Any calendar day the patient answered at least one survey question or marked at least one exercise complete. Opening a link without engaging does not count. Interaction days drive the device-supply codes. See [CPT code reference](../reference/cpt-code-reference.md).

## Do I really need an interactive contact every month?

For the treatment-management codes (`98979`, `98980`, `98981`), yes: at least one live phone, video, or in-person conversation per calendar month. Asynchronous messages do not qualify. See [Logging interactive contacts](../episodes/logging-interactive-contacts.md).

## Why are there two billing cards on the episode page?

RTM pays on two separate clocks: a rolling 30-day window for device supply, and the calendar month for treatment management. They measure different things over different periods, so the two cards rarely show matching numbers. See [Billing window progress](../billing/billing-window-progress.md).

## Can I edit a signed clinical note?

Not directly. A signed note is locked. Use **ReOpen Note** to unlock it, which clears the signature; make your edits, then sign again. See [Clinical notes and sign-off](../billing/clinical-notes.md).

## Does signing a note stop me from billing if I don't?

No. Signing is a documentation safeguard, not a billing gate. You can export or mark a claim billed with an unsigned note; many clinics make signing a habit anyway. See [Clinical notes and sign-off](../billing/clinical-notes.md).

## How do I add a patient who is not in RTMLink yet?

Create them from the Patients page, then enroll them in an episode. Front desk can also enroll an existing patient right from the check-in kiosk. See [Creating a new patient](../patients/creating-a-new-patient.md).

## A patient lost their check-in link. What do they do?

They request a fresh link using the phone number on file, or you resend it. See [Recovering portal access](../patient-portal/recovering-portal-access.md).

## Why can't a teammate see Billing or change settings?

Those depend on the person's role. Billing is for owners and billing staff; settings and team management are for the owner. See [Role permissions matrix](../reference/role-permissions-matrix.md).

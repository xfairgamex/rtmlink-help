# Glossary

The terms you will run into most in RTMLink, in plain language.

**RTM (Remote Therapeutic Monitoring)**: the Medicare and insurance-billable service RTMLink supports, where patients report on their symptoms between visits and providers track and treat remotely.

**Episode**: a single patient's RTM enrollment, from start to close. A patient has one active episode at a time.

**Check-in (survey)**: the set of questions a patient answers, usually daily. The app's sidebar says **Check-Ins**; forms sometimes say "survey". They are the same thing.

**Magic link**: the personal link texted or emailed to a patient that opens their check-in with no password.

**30-day window**: the rolling billing period for device-supply codes (`98977`, `98985`), measured in interaction days.

**Calendar month**: the billing period for treatment-management codes (`98979`, `98980`, `98981`), measured in provider minutes plus an interactive contact.

**Interaction day**: a calendar day on which the patient answered at least one survey question or marked at least one exercise complete. Merely opening a link does not count.

**Interactive contact**: a live, two-way conversation with the patient (phone, video, synchronous chat, or in-person). Required once per calendar month for treatment-management billing. Asynchronous messages do not qualify.

**Provider time**: billable minutes a provider logs reviewing a patient's data and managing their care.

**Billing suggestion / claim**: a billable item RTMLink derives from the patient's activity, shown on the Billing page for review, approval, and export.

**Clinical note**: the documentation attached to a billing claim, which a provider can sign to lock.

**HEP (Home Exercise Program)**: the exercises assigned to a patient, which they mark complete from their portal.

**Amendment**: a patient's correction to a past check-in; the original is preserved and the amendment is saved alongside it.

**Flag**: an alert raised when a patient's answer crosses a threshold you set, marking a response for follow-up.

**Featured question**: a question shown as its own column in the episode list, so you can scan a key value (like a pain score) at a glance.

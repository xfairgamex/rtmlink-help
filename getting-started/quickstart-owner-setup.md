# Quickstart: setting up your clinic

New to RTMLink as the clinic owner? This is your setup checklist, in the order that makes sense. Each step links to the full guide; you can do it in one sitting or across your first week.

## 1. Sign your agreement

When your clinic goes live, you sign the Subscription Agreement and BAA before anyone can use the app. See [Signing your subscription agreement](signing-your-subscription-agreement.md).

## 2. Set up your clinic profile

Open Clinic Settings and fill in your clinic's name and contact details, set your **timezone** (it governs every date in the app), and add your logo and brand colors so patients see your clinic. See [Clinic information and branding](../settings/clinic-information-and-branding.md), and [Clinic settings overview](../settings/clinic-settings-overview.md) for the rest of the page.

## 3. Add your team

Invite your providers and staff and give each the right role. They set their own passwords from a welcome link. See [Adding team members](../users/adding-team-members.md).

## 4. Connect your EHR (optional)

If you bill through DrChrono, set your EMR to DrChrono in Clinic Settings, then connect the account so patients and appointments sync automatically. See [Connecting DrChrono](../integrations/connecting-drchrono.md).

## 5. Check your billing rates

Review your per-code reimbursement rates so the dashboard's revenue figures reflect your clinic. See [Configuring billing rates](../billing/configuring-billing-rates.md).

## You're ready

With those done, your team can enroll patients and the daily rhythm begins. New providers should read [Quickstart: the provider daily loop](quickstart-provider-daily-loop.md); whoever handles claims should read [Quickstart: billing](quickstart-billing.md).

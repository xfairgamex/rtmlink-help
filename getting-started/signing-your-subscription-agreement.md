# Signing your subscription agreement

When your clinic goes live, RTMLink presents a **Subscription Agreement & BAA** (Business Associate Agreement) that the clinic owner signs before anyone can use the app. It is a one-time step, and it gates the whole clinic until it is done.

## What the owner does

The clinic owner is taken to the signing page, which has two parts:

1. **Review your agreement**: the Software Subscription Agreement and the Business Associate Agreement (Addendum A), prepared with your clinic's legal name, effective date, and rate. Read both.
2. **Sign electronically**: tick the box agreeing to sign electronically and confirming you are authorized to bind the clinic, type your **full legal name** as your signature and your **Title**, then click **Sign agreement**.

RTMLink confirms the signature, emails you an executed PDF copy, and lets you back into the app. You can download the executed copy again at any time.

## What other staff see

Until the owner signs, other team members see an **Almost there** waiting screen. They cannot use the clinic yet; only the owner can sign. Once the owner signs, everyone has normal access.

> Only the clinic owner can sign, and only a live clinic with a pending agreement sees this. Clinics that signed on paper, and trial or demo clinics, never see the signing screen. Patient surveys and the patient portal are never blocked by it.

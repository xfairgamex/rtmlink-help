# Signing your subscription agreement

When your clinic goes live, RTMLink presents a **Subscription Agreement & BAA** (Business Associate Agreement) that the clinic owner signs. It is a one-time step. Patient features stay locked until it is signed, but your team can keep setting up the clinic in the meantime.

## What the owner does

The clinic owner is taken to the signing page, which has two parts:

1. **Review your agreement**: the Software Subscription Agreement and the Business Associate Agreement (Addendum A), prepared with your clinic's legal name, effective date, and rate. Read both.
2. **Sign electronically**: tick the box agreeing to sign electronically and confirming you are authorized to bind the clinic, type your **Full legal name (your electronic signature)** and your **Title**, then click **Sign agreement**.

RTMLink confirms the signature, emails you an executed PDF copy, and unlocks the rest of the app. You can download the executed copy again at any time.

## What stays open while it is pending

Signing does not block your whole clinic. Until the owner signs, the pages that only set up your clinic's own content (and never show patient information) stay open for everyone who can normally reach them:

- **My Settings** and **Clinic Settings**
- **Users** and **Invoices**
- **Message Templates** and **Clinical Note Templates**
- **Check-In Templates**, **Exercises**, **HEP Templates**, and the **Video Library**
- **Support**

> Pages that are owner-only in normal use (such as **Clinic Settings** and **Invoices**) stay owner-only here too. Opening these setup pages early does not change who is allowed to see them.

Patient features stay locked until the agreement is signed. These include the dashboard, every patient and episode record, **Check-Ins**, **Messages**, **Billing**, and **Integrations**. The moment the owner signs, they all open for everyone.

> Patient check-ins and surveys already in progress are never affected while you wait. Flipping a clinic live does not interrupt a patient filling out their link.

## The agreement-pending banner

While the agreement is waiting to be signed, a slim **Agreement pending** banner appears on every clinic page:

- The clinic owner sees "Patient features unlock once you sign the Subscription Agreement & BAA" with a **Review & sign** button that opens the signing page.
- Everyone else sees "Patient features unlock once your clinic owner signs the RTMLink agreement."

## What other staff see first

If someone other than the owner tries to open a locked patient feature before the agreement is signed, they land on an **Almost there** waiting screen explaining that the clinic is live and one signature is still needed. Only the owner can sign. From there they can still use the open setup pages from the sidebar.

> Only the clinic owner can sign, and only a live clinic with a pending agreement sees any of this. Clinics that signed on paper, and trial or demo clinics, never see the signing screen or the banner.

# Logging in & account setup

This article covers signing in to RTMLink, setting your password the first time, changing it later, and getting back in if you're ever locked out.

## Your clinic's web address

RTMLink runs in your web browser — there's nothing to install. You sign in at your clinic's own web address, which looks like:

`yourclinic.rtmlink.com`

Your clinic administrator will give you the exact address. Bookmark it so it's easy to find. Everyone on your team — front desk, providers, billing — uses the same address.

## Setting up your account the first time

You don't create your own account. Your clinic administrator adds you, and the system emails you a **welcome message** with a secure sign-in link.

1. Open the welcome email and click the link.
2. You'll land on a **Welcome to [Your Clinic]** page.
3. Choose a password:
   - **New Password** — at least 8 characters.
   - **Confirm Password** — type the same password again.
4. Submit the form to set your password. You'll see a **Welcome aboard!** confirmation and you'll be signed in and taken to your dashboard.

> **The welcome link expires after 48 hours.** If you open it too late, it won't work — just ask your clinic administrator to send a new one.

> **Tip:** Each password box has a small eye icon. Click it to reveal what you typed so you can double-check before saving.

## Signing in day to day

Once your password is set, sign in from your clinic's web address with your **email** and **password**. That's all there is to it.

## Changing your password

When you're signed in, you can change your password any time:

1. Open the user menu (your name, in the sidebar) and click **My Settings**.
2. In the **Change Password** section, enter:
   - **Current Password** — the password you use now.
   - **New Password** — your new password (at least 8 characters).
   - **Confirm New Password** — type the new one again.
3. Click **Update Password**. You'll see **Password updated successfully.**

## If you can't sign in

There is no "forgot password" link on the sign-in page — this is intentional, to keep clinic accounts secure. If you've forgotten your password, or your welcome link expired, **contact your clinic administrator.** They can send you a fresh sign-in link so you can set a new password.

## Automatic sign-out

For security, RTMLink signs you out automatically after a stretch of inactivity. When that happens you'll see **You were logged out due to inactivity.** — just sign back in to pick up where you left off.

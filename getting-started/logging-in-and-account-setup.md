# Logging in & account setup

This article covers signing in to RTMLink, setting your password the first time, changing it later, and recovering access if you need to.

## Where to sign in

RTMLink runs in your web browser (nothing to install). The easiest place to sign in is:

`rtmlink.com/login`

Enter your email and password, then click **Log in**. If your account belongs to one clinic, you'll be forwarded to your clinic's dashboard automatically. You can also sign in directly from your clinic's own web address (for example, `yourclinic.rtmlink.com`), which takes you straight to your clinic's login page. Either path works. Once you're in, bookmark your clinic's dashboard URL so you can jump back without going through the login page each time.

> Your clinic administrator can give you the exact clinic address if you need it.

## Setting up your account the first time

You don't create your own account. Your clinic administrator adds you, and the system emails you a welcome message with a secure sign-in link.

1. Open the welcome email and click the link.
2. You'll land on a **Welcome to [Your Clinic]** page.
3. Choose a password:
   - **New Password:** at least 8 characters.
   - **Confirm Password:** type the same password again.
4. Click **Set Password & Sign In**. You'll see a **Welcome aboard!** notification and you'll be taken to your dashboard.

> **The welcome link expires after 48 hours.** If you open it too late, it won't work. Ask your clinic administrator to send a new one.

> **Tip:** Each password box has a small eye icon. Click it to reveal what you typed so you can double-check before saving.

## Signing in day to day

Once your password is set, sign in with your **Email address** and **Password**, then click **Log in**.

## Changing your password

When you're signed in, you can change your password any time:

1. Open the user menu (click your name in the bottom-left corner of the sidebar) and click **My Settings**.
2. In the **Change Password** section, enter:
   - **Current Password:** the password you use now.
   - **New Password:** your new password (at least 8 characters).
   - **Confirm New Password:** type the new one again.
3. Click **Update Password**. You'll see a **Password updated successfully** notification.

## Forgot your password?

If you've forgotten your password:

1. Go to the sign-in page and click **Forgot your password?** (below the **Password** field).
2. On the **Forgot password** page, enter your **Email Address** and click **Email password reset link**.
3. Check your inbox for a reset link, click it, then enter and confirm your new password and click **Reset password**.

> If the reset email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder. If it still doesn't turn up, the email address on your account may be different from the one you entered. Contact your clinic administrator to confirm which address is on file.

> **Welcome link expired?** If your original welcome link had already expired when you clicked it (links are valid for 48 hours), ask your clinic administrator to send a fresh one.

## If your account has been deactivated

If an administrator has deactivated your account, you'll see **Your account has been deactivated.** on the sign-in page and won't be able to log in. Contact your clinic administrator to restore access.

## Automatic sign-out

For security, RTMLink signs you out automatically after a stretch of inactivity. When that happens you'll see **You were logged out due to inactivity.** Sign back in to pick up where you left off.

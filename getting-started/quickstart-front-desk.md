# Quickstart: front desk

If you run the front desk, your RTMLink day centers on the check-in kiosk: a simple page that shows who is arriving and flags anyone not yet set up for monitoring. Here is the short version.

## 1. Open the kiosk

Open the private check-in link your clinic set up (bookmark it on the front-desk computer) and enter the PIN if there is one. It shows today's arrivals. See [Using the check-in kiosk](../front-desk/using-the-check-in-kiosk.md).

## 2. Catch the enrollments

Cards marked **Needs Enrollment** are patients who are not in RTM yet. Tap one to enroll them on the spot: pick their provider, survey, and schedule, and optionally send a welcome message. See [Enrolling and managing patients at check-in](../front-desk/enrolling-and-managing-at-check-in.md).

## 3. Handle walk-ins and new patients

Use the kiosk search to find anyone in your clinic, even without an appointment. If someone is brand new, add them from the Patients page first, then enroll them. See [Creating a new patient](../patients/creating-a-new-patient.md).

## 4. Keep the schedule current

If your clinic uses DrChrono, appointments sync automatically. Otherwise, a quick morning import keeps the day's list accurate. See [Viewing appointments](../appointments/viewing-appointments.md) and [Getting appointments into RTMLink](../appointments/syncing-appointments.md).

## That's it

Open the kiosk, enroll who needs it, keep the schedule tidy. If a patient can't open their check-in link, see [Recovering portal access](../patient-portal/recovering-portal-access.md).

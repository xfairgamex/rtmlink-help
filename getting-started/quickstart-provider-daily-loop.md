# Quickstart: the provider daily loop

As a provider, most days in RTMLink follow the same short loop. Once it's a habit it takes only a few minutes. Here it is end to end.

## 1. Start from your dashboard

Your dashboard's **Your Action Feed** shows what needs you today: surveys to review, claims to sign, and near-billable nudges, all scoped to your patients. See [Navigating the dashboard](navigating-the-dashboard.md).

## 2. Review the day's check-ins

Open your review page (from the dashboard pill, the daily email, or the Check-Ins queue) and work through each patient's responses, watching for flagged answers. See [Reviewing daily check-ins](../check-ins/reviewing-daily-check-ins.md), and [The Check-Ins queue](../check-ins/the-check-ins-queue.md) for the shared view.

## 3. Log your time and contacts as you go

Right on the review page, record the minutes you spent and, when you had a live call with a patient, log the interactive communication. Both count toward billing. See [Logging time and contacts during review](../check-ins/logging-time-during-review.md), and [Logging interactive contacts](../episodes/logging-interactive-contacts.md) for the qualifying rules.

## 4. Sign your claims

When a claim is ready, sign its clinical note to certify the documentation. Your dashboard's **Claims to sign** count and the weekly email tell you what's waiting. See [Clinical notes and sign-off](../billing/clinical-notes.md).

## That's the loop

Review, log, sign. Do it daily and your patients stay monitored and your months stay billable. Keep [Common issues](../troubleshooting/common-issues.md) handy for the behaviors that look odd but aren't.

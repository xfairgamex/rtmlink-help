# Welcome to RTMLink

RTMLink helps your clinic run a **Remote Therapeutic Monitoring (RTM)** program — keeping patients engaged between visits, watching their progress, and turning that activity into billable claims. This article explains what RTMLink is for and how the pieces fit together, so the rest of the help center makes sense.

## What is Remote Therapeutic Monitoring?

RTM is a service that lets your clinic monitor a patient's recovery **between appointments**. Patients answer a short daily check-in (a survey) about how they're doing — pain, mobility, how their home exercises went — and your providers review those responses and stay in touch. Because the patient is being actively monitored, the work qualifies for specific insurance billing codes.

RTMLink handles the moving parts: sending the daily check-ins, collecting responses, tracking which days a patient engaged, logging provider time, and telling you when a patient has met the thresholds to bill.

## The RTM workflow, end to end

Most of what you'll do in RTMLink follows this path:

1. **Add the patient.** Create the patient record with their contact details and consent.
2. **Enroll them in an episode.** An *episode* is one course of RTM monitoring, with a start date and an assigned provider.
3. **Daily check-ins go out.** The patient gets a link (by text or email) to a short survey each day. They tap it open on their phone — no app to install.
4. **Responses come back.** Providers review answers, watch for anyone who needs attention, and log their time and contacts.
5. **Billing adds up.** As the patient engages day by day, RTMLink counts toward the billing thresholds and surfaces a suggestion when a claim is ready.

You don't have to manage the timing or the counting by hand — RTMLink does that and shows you what needs your attention.

## A few terms you'll see often

- **Episode** — one patient's RTM enrollment, from start date to close.
- **Interaction day** — a calendar day the patient engaged (answered at least one survey question or marked a home exercise complete). Interaction days are what most RTM billing is counted in.
- **Interactive contact** — a real conversation (phone, video, or in person) between a provider and the patient. Some billing codes require it.
- **Billing window** — a 30-day stretch RTMLink uses to measure a patient's engagement for device-supply billing codes.

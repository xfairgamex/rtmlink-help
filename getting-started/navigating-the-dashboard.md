# Navigating the dashboard

The dashboard is the first thing you see after signing in. It's your clinic's home base: a quick read on how your Remote Therapeutic Monitoring program is doing and what needs your attention right now. This article walks through each part.

## Overview

At the top you'll see your clinic's name in the heading **[Your Clinic]'s RTMLink Dashboard**, with the subheading **Remote Therapeutic Monitoring Overview**. Below that, the page is built from a few sections that update on their own as your team works.

What you see on the dashboard depends on your role. Providers see a personal action feed scoped to their own patients. Clinic owners, staff, and billing staff see a clinic-wide attention panel instead. Everyone sees the key-metric cards and the onboarding checklist (until setup is complete).

![The RTMLink dashboard: the onboarding checklist, a row of key metrics, and the Needs Attention list.](../.gitbook/assets/getting-started/dashboard-overview.png)

## The numbers at a glance

A row of cards gives you the key figures for your program:

- **Unread Surveys:** responses waiting for review.
- **Active Episodes:** how many patients are currently being monitored.
- **Pending Surveys:** check-ins due today.
- **Response Rate:** the share of sent check-ins patients have answered this window.
- **Billable Minutes:** provider time logged this month.

These are live counts, so they're a fast way to take the pulse of the clinic without opening anything.

## Your Action Feed (providers)

If you have the Provider role, you'll see a **Your Action Feed** panel above the metric cards. It shows only your patients, so you can work through your list without sifting through the whole clinic's data.

The feed surfaces four things:

- **Surveys to review:** check-in responses from your patients waiting for your sign-off. Clicking the count badge takes you straight to the review page.
- **Claims to sign:** billing claims on your patients' episodes that still need a clinical note signed. Clicking takes you to your filtered claims list.
- **Revenue opportunities:** patients who are close to meeting (or at risk of missing) a billing threshold. Each row names the patient, the reason, and shows a quick-action button to address it.
- **At risk of dropping off:** patients who have had no billable activity for seven or more days. Their name links directly to their episode so you can act quickly.

If there's nothing to act on, the feed shows a confirmation that you're all caught up.

The **Enroll patient** link appears in the feed if you have permission to create episodes, giving you a quick way to start a new patient without navigating away.

## What Needs Attention (clinic owners, staff, billing staff)

If you have the Clinic Owner, Staff, or Billing Staff role, you'll see a **What Needs Attention** panel below the metric cards. It covers the whole clinic by default.

- **Provider filter:** a dropdown that lets you drill into a single provider's patients. Available when your clinic has providers with assigned episodes. Set it to **All providers** to return to the clinic-wide view.
- **Claims to sign:** the number of episodes across the clinic (or the selected provider's patients) with unsigned billing claims. Clicking takes you to the filtered claims list.
- **Pending check-ins:** unread survey responses awaiting review. Clicking takes you to the pending summaries page.
- **Revenue opportunities:** patients approaching or at risk of missing billing thresholds, listed with the reason and a quick-action button. Clinic owners and billing staff also see an estimated dollar value for each opportunity.
- **At risk of dropping off:** patients with no billable activity in the last seven or more days. Each row shows the patient's name, their assigned provider (in the clinic-wide view), and how many days since their last activity. You can act directly from the list: click **Pause** to pause the episode (you'll be prompted to pick a reason) or **Stop** to discharge the patient from RTM.

If there's nothing to act on, the panel shows a confirmation that the clinic is on track.

## Getting set up (new clinics)

When your clinic is brand new, the dashboard shows an **Onboarding Checklist** at the top to guide you through the first steps:

1. **First provider invited:** add at least one therapist or clinic owner so they can start logging interactions.
2. **First patient enrolled:** enroll a patient so they can begin receiving daily check-in surveys.
3. **First survey response received:** a patient has completed their first daily check-in.
4. **First interactive contact logged:** a provider has documented their first qualifying phone, video, or in-person contact. This is required for treatment-management billing codes.
5. **First billing suggestion generated:** RTM thresholds have been met for a patient and a billing suggestion is ready for review.

Each incomplete step shows a **Take action** link that drops you directly onto the right screen. Once every milestone is checked off, the checklist disappears automatically.

## Finding your way around

Everything in RTMLink is reached from the **sidebar** on the left.

- Near the top are the screens you'll use most: **Patients**, **Episodes**, **Messages**, **Check-Ins**, **Appointments**, and **Billing**.
- Below those are grouped sections you'll open less often: **Templates** (your reusable check-in and message templates, exercises, and more) and **Settings** (clinic settings, your team, integrations, and your own **My Settings**).
- Your name opens the user menu, where you'll find **My Settings** and the sign-out option.

What appears in your sidebar depends on your role. See [Understanding your role](understanding-your-role.md). You can collapse the sidebar to give yourself more room whenever you like.

# Navigating the dashboard

The dashboard is the first thing you see after signing in. It's your clinic's home base: a quick read on how your Remote Therapeutic Monitoring program is doing today and what needs your attention. This article walks through each part.

## Overview

At the top you'll see your clinic's name — **[Your Clinic] Dashboard** — with the subheading **Remote Therapeutic Monitoring Overview**. Below that, the page is built from a few simple sections that update on their own as your team works.

![The RTMLink dashboard: the onboarding checklist, a row of key metrics, and the Needs Attention list.](../.gitbook/assets/getting-started/dashboard-overview.png)

## The numbers at a glance

A row of cards gives you the key figures for your program:

- **Unread Surveys** — your review queue: responses that haven't been looked at yet.
- **Active Episodes** — how many patients are currently being monitored.
- **Pending Surveys** — check-ins due today.
- **Response Rate** — the share of sent check-ins patients have answered this window.
- **Billable Minutes** — provider time logged this month.

These are live counts, so they're a fast way to take the pulse of the clinic without opening anything.

## Needs Attention

The **Needs Attention** list surfaces the active episodes that need a look, most urgent first. An episode shows up here when, for example:

- a patient is flagged as **high risk**,
- a patient **hasn't responded** in a few days,
- a billing window is **about to close**, or
- a patient is **behind pace** for their monitoring goal.

Each item names the patient and links straight to their episode, so you can go from "who needs me?" to the right screen in one click.

> If **Needs Attention** is empty, that's good news — nothing is currently at risk.

## Quick Actions

The **Quick Actions** area gives you one-click shortcuts to the things you do most, such as **Enroll Patient**. Use it to jump straight into a common task instead of hunting through the menus.

## Getting set up (new clinics)

When your clinic is brand new, the dashboard shows a **setup checklist** to guide you through the first steps — things like having your business agreement on file, inviting your first provider, enrolling your first patient, and receiving your first survey response. Once those basics are done, the checklist disappears on its own and makes room for your day-to-day numbers.

## Finding your way around

Everything in RTMLink is reached from the **sidebar** on the left.

- Near the top are the screens you'll use most: **Patients**, **Episodes**, **Messages**, **Check-Ins**, **Appointments**, and **Billing**.
- Below those are grouped sections you'll open less often — **Templates** (your reusable check-in and message templates, exercises, and more) and **Settings** (clinic settings, your team, integrations, and your own **My Settings**).
- Your name opens the user menu, where you'll find **My Settings** and the sign-out option.

What appears in your sidebar depends on your role — see [Understanding your role](understanding-your-role.md). You can collapse the sidebar to give yourself more room whenever you like.

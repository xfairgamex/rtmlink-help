# Understanding your role

Every person on your clinic's team is given a **role** when their account is created. Your role decides what you can see and do in RTMLink — and which sections show up in your sidebar. This article explains the five roles so you know what to expect from yours.

## How roles work

Your clinic administrator assigns your role when they add you. If you ever need different access, ask them — only the **Clinic Owner** can add team members and change roles.

If a section described elsewhere in this help center doesn't appear for you, your role probably doesn't include it. That's normal — it's not a bug.

## The five roles

- **Clinic Owner** — Full run of the clinic. Manages patients, episodes, check-ins, billing, clinic settings, and the team itself. This is the role for clinic owners and administrators.
- **Provider** — The clinician's view. Sees every patient and episode, enrolls patients into monitoring, reviews survey responses, and logs their own treatment time. Doesn't touch billing, settings, or the team list.
- **Staff** — The front-desk / coordinator role. Adds and edits patients, creates episodes and enrolls patients, sends check-ins, and logs time. Doesn't see billing or settings.
- **Billing Staff** — Focused on claims. Reviews patients and episodes (read-only), logs and reviews time across the clinic, and manages everything in **Billing**. Doesn't edit patient or clinical records.
- **Auditor** — Read-only across the whole clinic, including the activity log. Can look at everything but change nothing — ideal for compliance reviews.

## What each role can do

This table shows access by area. "Full" means create, edit, and manage; "View" means look but not change; "—" means the area isn't available to that role.

| Area | Clinic Owner | Provider | Staff | Billing Staff | Auditor |
|------|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| Dashboard | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Patient records | Full | View | Add & edit | View | View |
| Episodes (monitoring) | Full | Manage | Create & enroll | View | View |
| Survey responses | Full | Review | — | — | View |
| Check-in templates | Full | View | — | — | View |
| Time tracking | All | Own | View & log | View & log | View |
| Messages | Full | Send | Send | — | View |
| Billing & claims | Full | — | — | Full | View |
| Clinic settings | Full | — | — | — | — |
| Team & roles | Full | — | — | — | — |
| Activity log | View | — | — | — | View |

> **Note:** "Time tracking → Own" means a provider can log and review their own time; "All" means time across the whole clinic.

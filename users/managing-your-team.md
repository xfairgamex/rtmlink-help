# Managing your team

Open **Settings** > **Users** to see everyone in your clinic: their name, email, role, status, and last login. From here you keep the roster current as people join, change roles, or leave.

## Editing a member

Use the actions menu on a member's row and choose **Edit** to update their name, credentials, email, or role. Role changes take effect immediately.

## Resending a welcome link

If a new member's sign-in link expired before they used it, choose **Send welcome email** from their row. RTMLink confirms it will email a fresh sign-in link and invalidate the previous one, so only the newest link works.

## Deactivating and reactivating

Team members are never deleted; instead you deactivate them, which preserves their history and everything they logged.

- **Deactivate** (on an active member's row) blocks their access: the next time they try to use RTMLink they are signed out and told the account has been deactivated. You cannot deactivate yourself.
- **Reactivate** restores access to a deactivated member.

Both actions ask for confirmation and update the member's **Status** badge between **Active** and **Inactive**.

> Deactivate anyone who leaves the clinic rather than trying to delete them. Their logged time, signed notes, and audit trail must stay intact for billing and compliance.

## Who can manage the team

Team management, including inviting, editing, and deactivating, is a Clinic Owner task.

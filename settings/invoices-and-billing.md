# Invoices and billing

This is about your clinic's subscription to RTMLink, separate from the insurance claims you bill for patients. Open **Settings** > **Invoices** to see your plan, payment method, and invoice history.

## Your plan

The top of the page shows your current plan. Plan changes are handled by the RTMLink team for now: the **Adjust plan** button is turned off (it reads "Plan management is coming soon"), and the page points you to **Contact us** if you want to discuss your plan details.

## Payment method

The **Payment** section shows the card or account on file. Click **Update** (or **Add** if none is set) to manage it through the secure Stripe billing portal.

With a payment method on file, future invoices are auto-paid with that account. Without one, RTMLink emails you the invoice with a payment link instead.

## How invoices are paid

RTMLink sends your invoice emails itself, so an email from RTMLink is your clinic's only invoice notice (there is no separate email from a payment processor). Each invoice email goes to your clinic's billing contact and lists what you are being charged for.

You will see one of three emails:

- **Your RTMLink invoice for [period]** when a new invoice is issued.
- **Payment received for your [period] RTMLink invoice** once a payment clears.
- **Action needed: payment for your [period] RTMLink invoice** if an automatic payment fails.

How the payment itself is handled depends on the arrangement your clinic set up with RTMLink. Most clinics are on the standard arrangement, where a saved payment method is charged automatically and, if none is saved, the invoice email carries a **Pay this invoice** button. Two other arrangements exist:

- **Pay by check:** every invoice is emailed with check-remittance instructions (who to make the check payable to and the invoice number for the memo line), even if you have a payment method saved. A member of the RTMLink team records the payment once it arrives.
- **Pay by card:** invoices are paid on a card-only page, and every invoice includes a separate **Card processing fee** line (2.9% plus $0.30 at current rates) on top of the service total.

> **Note:** These arrangements are set up with RTMLink, not chosen on this page. If you are unsure which one your clinic is on, or you want to switch, email [support@rtmlink.com](mailto:support@rtmlink.com) or open a ticket at [support.rtmlink.com/forms/rtmlink-support](https://support.rtmlink.com/forms/rtmlink-support).

## Invoices

The **Invoices** section lists each invoice with its date, total, and status (**Paid**, **Open**, and so on). Use **View** to open the invoice, and **Load more** to see older ones. A new clinic simply shows **No invoices yet**.

## Requesting cancellation

The **Cancellation** section has a **Cancel** button that requests cancellation and confirms first. It does not end service instantly; instead, a member of the RTMLink team reaches out to discuss your account, and the page shows **Cancellation requested** in the meantime.

## Who manages billing

Subscription billing is a Clinic Owner responsibility, and only the Clinic Owner can open the **Invoices** page. It is not available to providers, staff, billing staff, or auditors. (This is your clinic's subscription to RTMLink, separate from the insurance claims your billing staff work on under **Billing**.)

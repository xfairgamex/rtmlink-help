# Understanding RTM billing

RTMLink watches your patients' activity and your team's logged time, and turns qualifying work into billing claims automatically. This article explains the model behind the **Billing** page so the numbers always make sense.

## The two billing clocks

RTM pays on two separate schedules, and RTMLink tracks them separately:

- **Device-supply codes** are earned over each episode's rolling **30-day window** and are based on **interaction days**: unique calendar dates on which the patient engaged (answered a check-in question or completed an exercise). These claims surface only **after a window closes**, which is why a mid-window episode shows progress but no device-supply claim yet.
- **Treatment-management codes** are earned over the **calendar month** and are based on **provider minutes** plus at least one **interactive contact**. These are counted **per patient, not per episode**: if a patient has more than one episode in a month, their minutes and contacts are combined and RTMLink creates **one** monthly treatment claim, attached to the most recent episode with activity.

> **Why you sometimes see one claim where you expected two:** a patient with two open episodes gets a single treatment-management claim each month. The episode page's **This Calendar Month** card shows the patient-wide total whenever another episode contributed activity.

## The CPT codes

| Code | What it pays for | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `98975` | Initial setup and patient education | Once per episode. Your clinic chooses how many interaction days to wait before it surfaces (see [Configuring billing rates](configuring-billing-rates.md)). |
| `98976` | Device supply, respiratory system | 16+ interaction days in a 30-day window |
| `98977` | Device supply, musculoskeletal system | 16+ interaction days in a 30-day window |
| `98985` | Device supply, partial window | 2 to 15 interaction days in a 30-day window |
| `98979` | Treatment management, first 10 minutes | Calendar month, requires an interactive contact |
| `98980` | Treatment management, first 20 minutes | Calendar month, requires an interactive contact |
| `98981` | Treatment management, each additional 20 minutes | Same claim as the month's `98980` |

## Reading the Billing page

Click **Billing** in the left sidebar. At the top, three cards summarize where things stand: **Ready to Bill** (claims awaiting billing), **Est. Revenue** (the total those claims are worth at your configured rates), and **Billed This Month**.

Below them, tabs split the list: **Ready to Bill**, **Billed**, **Rejected**, and **All**. Each row is **one claim**, grouped by patient:

- **Billing Note** tells you which clock produced the claim: Setup, Device Supply for a numbered window, or Treatment Mgmt for a named month.
- **Clinical Note** shows the documentation state: **Missing**, **Draft**, or **Signed**. See [Clinical notes and sign-off](clinical-notes.md).
- **CPT Codes** shows every code on the claim. A month's `98980` and its `98981` add-ons are one claim, submitted together, so they share a row.
- **Qualifiers** shows the evidence at a glance: interaction-day counts for device-supply claims, or minutes and contacts for treatment claims.
- **Status** and **Est. Amount** track the claim through your workflow and what it is worth.

## Where claims come from

Claims are generated by RTMLink as episodes and windows qualify; you never create them by hand on this page. The empty state says it plainly: claims appear here when episodes and windows qualify for RTM codes. There is one exception: users with billing approval can add a **manual claim** from an episode's **Billing Claims** tab for a code the system did not derive. See [Billing claims and suggestions](billing-suggestions.md).

## Role permissions

| Action | Clinic Owner | Provider | Staff | Billing Staff | Auditor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| View the Billing page | Yes | Yes | No | Yes | Yes |

| Configure rates | Yes | No | No | No | No |

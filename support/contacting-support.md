# Contacting support

Every page in RTMLink has a **Help and support** icon in the top bar, next to your user menu. It is the single door to everything: these guides, your support tickets, and a call with our team.

## The Help menu

Click the help icon to open the menu:

- **Support docs** opens this help center in a new tab.
- **View support tickets** opens your ticket list. A badge on this item counts tickets **awaiting your reply**.
- **New ticket** starts a fresh request.
- **Schedule a call** books time with the support team (when your clinic has scheduling available).

## Opening a ticket

Choose **New ticket**, give it a subject, and describe what you need under **How can we help?**. You can **CC colleagues** so they see the thread from the start, and add **Attachments** such as a screenshot.

There is no email round-trip: the ticket lives in RTMLink, and you reply on the thread. When our team responds, you get a notification in the app's bell so nothing gets lost in an inbox.

## Tracking your tickets

**View support tickets** lists your requests with the ticket number, who opened it, its status, and last activity. Statuses are:

- **Open**: with our support team.
- **Awaiting reply**: waiting on you. These are the ones the Help menu badge counts.
- **Closed**: resolved. Reply on the thread if it needs reopening.

> **Who sees which tickets.** Clinic Owners see all of their clinic's tickets. Other team members see the tickets they opened or were CC'd on, so a provider's request stays between them and support.

## Booking a call

**Schedule a call** opens a booking panel showing **Available times** in your clinic's timezone. Pick a slot and **Confirm booking**; you will get the meeting details for the call.

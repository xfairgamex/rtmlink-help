# Previewing a survey

Before a check-in goes out, you can walk through it exactly as a patient will: the same consent screen, questions, scales, wording, and home exercises, with your clinic's branding. Previewing is the quickest way to catch a confusing question, a missing answer option, or consent wording that needs a second look.

## Two ways to preview

There are two entry points, and they start at different places.

**Preview the survey by itself.** Click **Preview Survey** from any of these:

- The actions menu on a survey's row in **Check-In Templates**.
- The top of a survey when you're **viewing** it.
- The top of a survey while you're **editing** it.

This opens the survey step on its own in a **new tab**, so you can keep editing in the original tab and refresh the preview to check your changes.

**Preview the whole patient experience.** To see the full flow a patient walks, not just the survey:

1. Open **Clinic Settings** in the left sidebar (under **Settings**).
2. Find the **Branding** section.
3. Under **Preview the patient experience**, click **Open patient preview**.

This opens in a new tab and starts at the first screen a patient actually meets, then steps forward through the rest of the flow.

> **You need at least one active survey.** If your clinic has no active survey yet, the preview link is replaced with a note to create a survey first.

## What the preview walks through

The full preview mirrors your clinic's own settings, so it shows a step only when a patient would see it:

- **Consent screen** (only when **Require RTM Program Consent** is turned on, in the **Patient Consent** section of Clinic Settings). This is the same consent screen a patient meets, but it's inert: the **I Agree** button just moves you to the survey, and **I don't want to participate** is disabled. When consent is on, the settings entry also offers a **Skip to the survey** link if you'd rather jump straight in.
- **The survey.** Each question in order, with the exact controls the patient uses: the 0 to 10 scale with your end labels, multiple-choice options, yes/no buttons, number fields, and text boxes, all under your clinic's branding. Every question is optional, so you can click through freely.
- **Home exercise program** (only when your clinic has **Exercises** turned on). Where a question is linked to the home program, a **Start your exercises now** button appears, and the walkthrough shows the exercises from your clinic's library.

The survey-only entry point (**Preview Survey**) skips straight to the survey step, even when consent is turned on.

> **Nothing is saved.** The whole preview is a safe, read-only walkthrough: agreeing to consent, answering questions, or tapping through exercises doesn't create a consent record, a response, or an exercise log, doesn't count as an interaction, and doesn't affect any patient. It's purely for your review.

## Preview vs. the patient's real link

The preview is for **signed-in clinic staff**. It is not the patient's link. Patients receive their own personal link by SMS or email, which opens their real check-in, saves their answers, records a response, and logs completed exercises. See [Understanding survey responses](understanding-survey-responses.md) and [Taking a survey](../patient-portal/taking-a-survey.md) for the patient's side.
